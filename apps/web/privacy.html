1. Who is responsible

Wally (wally.luminull.com) is a shared to-do board for individuals and small teams. It is a free, non-commercial personal project operated under the name Luminull (“we”, “us”). Luminull is the controller responsible for the processing of your personal data in Wally within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

For anything about your personal data, including requests to exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@luminull.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because the legal requirements for one (Art. 37 GDPR) do not apply to us.

2. What data we process

We only process the data needed to provide Wally. Depending on how you use it, that is:

Account data

  • Your email address, display name and whether your email address is verified. You provide these when you sign up, or they come from Google if you sign in with Google (see section 3).
  • A random Wally user ID, and the identifier of your login at our sign-in provider, which links the two.
  • Your language and your settings in the app, such as theme, colour palette, board view and filter choices, the board you last opened, and whether the assistant may apply its suggestions automatically.
  • When your account was created and last changed.

Your password, if you sign up with email and password, is handled only by our sign-in provider Auth0. Wally never sees or stores it.

Content you create

  • Boards (their names), notes (title, description, tags, custom properties, subtasks, due dates, links between notes, assignee and visibility).
  • A short history for each note: who created it or moved it, and when. This lets members of a board see how a note got where it is.

Notes are free text. Please do not enter sensitive information about yourself or others (for example health data) into Wally; it is not designed for that.

Sharing and membership

  • Who is a member of which board, with their role, and when they were invited or joined.
  • The email address of people you invite to a board, including people who do not have a Wally account yet.
  • Share links you create, with their role and expiry.

Assistant data

If you use the built-in assistant, we process your questions, the assistant's answers and suggestions, the board content it reads to answer you, your decisions on its suggestions, and how much you used it (number of requests, tokens and cost), so we can enforce fair-use limits.

Connected apps

If you connect an external AI app (for example ChatGPT or Claude) to Wally, we store a record of the connection: the app's identifier, the permissions you granted, and when the connection was created or revoked, plus a log of the changes it made on your behalf. We never store the app's access tokens in these records.

Technical data

When your browser talks to Wally, our hosting providers necessarily process your IP address, the requested address (URL), the time, your browser's user agent and similar connection data. Wally's own server log records only the request method and path and whether it succeeded, never your IP address. Our cloud provider's platform log does record the IP address and user agent of each request (see section 8 for how long).

When you contact us

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer you.

What we do not collect: We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, session recordings or third-party fonts or scripts. We do not collect your location, contacts, or any data from other apps on your device.

3. Signing in with Google

You can create a Wally account and sign in with your Google account. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google asks for your permission and then shares the following with our sign-in provider Auth0, using only the basic scopes openid, email and profile:

  • your name,
  • your email address and whether Google has verified it,
  • your Google account identifier, and
  • your profile picture address and language setting, if your Google profile has them.

How we use it. Wally uses your name, email address and verification status only to create your account, sign you in, show your name to members of boards you share, and let you accept invitations sent to your address. Wally does not use your profile picture. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google data.

How we store and share it. Your name, email address and the link to your Google login are kept with your account, in our sign-in provider Auth0 and in Wally's database in the EU, until you delete your account. We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with anyone except the service providers named in section 5 who process it for us, and the other members of boards you join.

Wally's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Wally's access to your Google account at any time in your Google account settings. This does not delete your Wally account; to do that, use Account and data in the app. Google's own processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Who receives your data

Other Wally users

When you are a member of a board, the other members can see your display name and email address (so they can assign notes to you), and everything on that board except private notes. A private note is visible only to the person who created it. If you invite someone, the members of that board see the invited email address. Anyone with a share link can join the board with the role the owner chose.

Our service providers (processors)

6. AI features and connected apps

The built-in assistant

When you use Wally's assistant, the question you ask and the board content it needs to answer are sent to Google's Vertex AI service in the EU, which runs the language model and returns the answer to us. Google processes this data as our processor and, under its Google Cloud terms, does not use it to train its models. The assistant only proposes changes: it applies them only when you accept them, or automatically if you have turned that setting on. It makes no decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR).

AI output can be wrong. Please check suggestions before relying on them.

External AI apps (MCP connections)

You can connect an external AI app such as ChatGPT or Claude to Wally. Once you approve the connection, that app can read the boards you have access to (but never other people's private notes) and make the changes your role allows. The app's provider receives this content as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy and your settings there; we have no control over how it stores or uses the data. You can disconnect an app at any time under Connected apps in Wally. This stops future access but cannot delete copies the provider already holds.

7. Transfers outside the EU

We store Wally's data in the EU. Some of our service providers are US companies, or use subprocessors outside the European Economic Area, and Cloudflare handles requests at whichever of its data centres is nearest you. Where personal data is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, the transfer is protected by:

  • the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), for providers certified under it, or
  • the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR), which form part of our providers' data processing agreements.

You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

When you delete your account, it takes effect immediately: your login at Auth0, your account, the boards you own (for all their members), every note you created on any board, and your memberships are deleted. Notes you were only assigned to stay on their board, unassigned. Records other members keep of their own activity can still mention you until they expire, at most 30 days later. Our providers' backups are overwritten on their own schedules.

9. Cookies and browser storage

Wally does not use advertising or tracking cookies, so it shows no cookie banner. It stores only what is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for (§ 25(2) TDDDG):

  • in your browser's local storage: your language, theme and colour palette, a flag that hides a hint you have seen, and the time of your last sign-out (so other open tabs sign out too);
  • from our sign-in provider Auth0: a cookie that remembers that you are signed in, and temporary data that carries the sign-in across the redirect to the login page. Auth0's login page sets its own session cookies to keep you signed in.

You can delete this data at any time in your browser settings; you will then be signed out and your display preferences reset.

10. Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk (Art. 32 GDPR), including: encryption of all connections (HTTPS/TLS); a database that only Wally's server can reach, with every request checked against your role on the board; sign-in through a dedicated identity provider, with verified email addresses; share links that cannot be guessed and expire; minimal logging; and limits on requests to prevent abuse. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify the authority and you as the GDPR requires.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15);
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • erasure (Art. 17);
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21).

Most of this you can do yourself in the app: Account and data lets you download all your data as a JSON file and delete your account; you can edit or delete your notes and boards at any time. For anything else, email privacy@luminull.com. We answer within one month (Art. 12(3) GDPR). We may need to confirm your identity first, normally by replying from the email address of your account.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state where you live or work, or where you think an infringement took place. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

12. Other information

Children. Wally is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly process their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Automated decisions. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

People invited to a board. If someone invited you to a Wally board by email and you have no account, we hold only your email address and the invitation, because the person who invited you entered it. It is deleted automatically 30 days after the invitation. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

13. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when Wally or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
